CEO Profile

Meet Our Leadership Team

Chao Pang

Founder & CEO

Biography

Chao Pang, male, born in July 1989, holds a Master's degree in Biomedical Engineering. As the founder and CEO of GreatWallMed, he focuses on the R&D and innovation of medical devices, leading the team to develop multiple medical devices with independent intellectual property rights, including ultrasound scanners, middle ear function analyzers, and ophthalmic OCT products.

Education Background

  • Sep 2012 - Jul 2015, Peking Union Medical College, Biomedical Engineering, Master's Degree
  • Sep 2008 - Jul 2012, Chongqing University of Posts and Telecommunications, Biomedical Engineering, Bachelor's Degree

Work Experience

  • Jul 2015 - Present, Tianjin Maida Medical Technology Co., Ltd., R&D Engineer / Project Lead
  • 2026 - Present, Founder & CEO of GreatWallMed

Key Projects Led

  • R&D, certification and medical device registration of bladder ultrasound scanner
  • R&D and domestic registration of ultrasound middle ear function analyzer
  • R&D and clinical evaluation of ophthalmic optical coherence tomography scanner (OCT)
  • Handheld bladder ultrasound scanner and system R&D (Tianjin Key Science and Technology Support Project)

AI Technology Applications

  • AI-assisted Diagnostic System: deep learning-based medical image analysis and diagnostic support
  • Intelligent Ultrasound Imaging Algorithm: using AI to optimize ultrasound image quality and resolution
  • Machine Learning Prediction Model: disease risk assessment and prognosis prediction based on patient data
  • Natural Language Processing: intelligent analysis of medical documents and automatic report generation
  • Computer Vision: medical image segmentation, lesion detection and feature extraction

Research Achievements

  • Published multiple papers in core journals, including several SCI-indexed papers
  • Obtained multiple national invention patents
  • Led and participated in multiple provincial and ministerial-level research projects
