About GreatWallMed

Founded on March 12, 2026, Great Wall Medical Technology (Tianjin) Co., Ltd. is a high-tech company focused on the R&D and manufacturing of medical devices, dedicated to providing innovative and reliable medical equipment solutions for the global healthcare industry.

Our Mission

Through innovative technology and quality products, we advance medical diagnosis and treatment, improve patient quality of life, and contribute to the global healthcare industry.

Core Business

  • R&D and manufacturing of ultrasound instruments
  • Neonatal middle ear function testing (acoustic immittance)

Global Market

Our products are primarily targeted at the international market, selling to countries and regions across Europe, North America, and Asia-Pacific, earning trust and acclaim from global customers.

Technical Advantages

  • Independently developed core technology
  • Strict quality control system
  • Continuous innovation capability
  • Professional technical support team
  • Comprehensive after-sales service network
